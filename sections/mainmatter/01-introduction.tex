\chapter{Introduction}
\label{chap:introduction}

Thresholding is all around us.
Perfect privacy is nice, but does not survive the constraints of the real world.
You almost always want some additional utility on top of your privacy (otherwise what's the point?).
The most fundamental form of privacy is the threshold.
Here are some scheme that do this, but where is the deployment?
Answer: efficieny. It is a compromise to be private, so need to make as efficient as possible.
Our results: two new private protocols, as well as building blocks for future ones (MDSS).
